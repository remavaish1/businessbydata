Goals, KPIs & Scorecards

Choose measures your team can act on.

Explore the workshop
  • Who it’s for — Business owners, leaders and managers who set targets or track performance.
  • What you’ll do — Build a draft scorecard for one business goal, such as reducing customer returns.
  • What you’ll learn — Choose KPIs, define how to calculate them and set targets and thresholds for action.

Pairs well with Reviewing Performance & Measuring Impact. Set your measures up front, then use them to assess whether your initiative worked.

Reading & Trusting Data

Check what your reports can tell you.

Explore the workshop
  • Who it’s for — Leaders who use reports or dashboards to make decisions.
  • What you’ll do — Review a business report and identify which conclusions its charts and figures support.
  • What you’ll learn — Spot data gaps, make fair comparisons and recognise normal variation.

Pairs well with Diagnosing Business Performance. Check that the comparison is fair, then investigate what drove the change.

Diagnosing Business Performance

Find out what drove a change in results.

Explore the workshop
  • Who it’s for — Leaders who need to explain why a KPI moved.
  • What you’ll do — Investigate a rise in customer returns. Break it down by product or channel and quantify the biggest contributors.
  • What you’ll learn — Identify the biggest drivers, test possible explanations and decide what to check first.

Pairs well with Reading & Trusting Data. Check the underlying report before drawing conclusions about performance.

Comparing Options & Making Decisions

Compare options and see what could change your decision.

Explore the workshop
  • Who it’s for — Leaders weighing options with different costs, benefits and risks.
  • What you’ll do — Build a side-by-side comparison of two options and see how your choice changes if demand or costs shift.
  • What you’ll learn — Estimate break-even points, test assumptions and explain the trade-offs behind your decision.

Pairs well with Reviewing Performance & Measuring Impact. Choose an option, then plan how to test it before a wider rollout.

Reviewing Performance & Measuring Impact

Decide whether to expand, adjust or stop an initiative.

Explore the workshop
  • Who it’s for — Leaders who want to know whether a pilot, initiative or operational change worked.
  • What you’ll do — Review a pilot against its baseline and a comparison group. Check for side effects and decide what to do next.
  • What you’ll learn — Define success, judge how much of the change you can attribute to the pilot and agree who owns the follow-up.

Pairs well with Goals, KPIs & Scorecards or Comparing Options & Making Decisions. Define success before launch or compare options before choosing what to pilot.

  • Who it’s for — Teams that prepare recurring reports in Excel or Power BI, with an advanced track for analysts.

AI for Workplace Productivity

Explore the workshop
  • Who it’s for — Teams that want to use AI for everyday work, with an advanced track for teams ready to automate recurring tasks.
  • What you’ll do — Summarise meeting notes, extract actions and draft a follow-up email. In AI Agents & Workflow Automation, build an agent that gathers updates from approved sources and drafts a weekly status report for review.

Primary · AI Awareness

Explore AI through facilitator-led live demos.

Explore the workshop
  • Who it’s for — Primary school pupils, with examples adapted to their age.
  • What you’ll do — Watch live AI demos and share ideas as the facilitator creates a character, image or story.
  • What you’ll learn — Spot AI in everyday life, question surprising answers and know when to stop, think and ask a trusted adult.

Secondary · Data & AI Literacy

Explore data, AI and how people use both in different careers.

Explore the workshop
  • Who it’s for — Secondary students exploring data and AI in school, daily life and different careers.
  • What you’ll do — Read charts, check an AI answer against reliable sources and explore how people use data and AI in sport, business and design. In Solve a Real Problem, investigate a question from school or community life.
  • What you’ll learn — Spot misleading claims, draw conclusions from evidence and recognise questions about privacy and fairness.

Higher Education · Applied Data & AI

Apply data and AI to coursework, research and career challenges.

Explore the workshop
  • Who it’s for — College and university students using data and AI for coursework, research or career preparation.
  • What you’ll do — Analyse a dataset, use AI to explore a question and present your findings. In Solve a Real Problem, apply those skills to a challenge relevant to your course or a career you’re exploring.
